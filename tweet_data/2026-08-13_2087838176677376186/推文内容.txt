FY2025 revenue: £97.3m

They're rapidly growing again in the optical sector, out of bank debt/distressed liquidation state, and remains a critical chokepoint in Western optical supply chains. 

Now... with new agreements with your leading photonics companies for revenue growth, and players like Macom serving as a soft backstop.  

After a thesis gets validated, typically I just sit back and enjoy growth of a business.
